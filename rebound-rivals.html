Rebound Rivals

Air hockey meets Rocket League in this chaotic couch co-op vehicular sports game with bouncy physics. Free to play on Steam.

Play free on Steam Watch trailer

About

Blast into action with your trusty air cannon with gameplay inspired by Air Hockey and additions like traps and power-ups. Play with your friends in the classic game or go all out in the destructive chaos mode where you have more than your opponent to worry about.

  • Air cannon momentum movement
  • Bounce off everything, including your opponent
  • Customisable vehicles
  • Multiple maps, traps and power-ups
  • 2 to 4 players in 1v1 or 2v2
