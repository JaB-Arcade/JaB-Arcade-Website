Who We Are

JaB Arcade is a two-person indie studio founded on 25th October 2024 by Jake Goodes and Bradley Curtis. The name comes from our initials, and the idea has always been the same: take a jab at making the games we'd want to play ourselves.

Jake is the builder, coding and using tools to make everything work. Bradley is the artist and creative, coming up with the concepts we bring to life.

Whatever we make, we make it as a team.
